Contribute

Help keep it free

Keeping this platform free and accessible to everyone costs ₹10,000 each year. If it has been useful to you, please consider contributing. Every contribution, large or small, helps us keep it running.

How it works

  • Any amount is welcome. ₹10,000 is the yearly target, not a limit.
  • We keep collecting all year. Once a year's ₹10,000 is met, new contributions count towards the next year.
  • Contributions are voluntary and pay for running costs. They don't buy anything.
  • Online payments will open when the platform launches.

Questions

Read the refund policy, or write to directorybridges@gmail.com.
