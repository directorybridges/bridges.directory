Privacy policy

What we collect, why, who can see it, and how to take it back.

Last updated 15 November 2026

1. Who we are

Bridges.directory (“we”) is run by [legal name of the team or entity, to be confirmed]. Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, we are the data fiduciary for the personal data described here.

2. What we collect

  • Your listing: name, institute and campus, branch, batch or expected graduation year, what you’re happy to help with, your avatar, an optional portfolio link and, for alumni, your experience.
  • Your Google account: when you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address, and Google’s confirmation that the address is yours. We never see your Google password.
  • Contact details: your institute email (students), and for alumni, a contact email if you set one.
  • Verification: the certificate you upload or the referral code you enter, and a record of the method and the outcome.
  • Volunteers: your mobile number.
  • Contributions: the amount, the date and the name you choose to show, or “Anonymous”. Payments are handled by our payment provider; we never see or store card or UPI details.
  • Activity: which contacts you have opened, to apply the weekly limit. With your consent, anonymous page counts.

3. Why we use it

To verify and list you, to let signed-in members contact you within the weekly limit, to run the volunteer programme, to process contributions, to keep the platform free of spam and misuse, and, only if you allow it, to understand which pages help.

4. Consent

We process your data on the basis of the consent you give when you sign up, and for uses the law permits without consent, such as responding to legal requests. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account; we then stop listing you.

5. Who can see what

  • Listings are visible only to signed-in, verified members, not to search engines or visitors.
  • Your email and portfolio link are shown only when a member chooses to open your contact, which uses one of their five contacts that week.
  • Volunteers see only name, institute, branch and year from a certificate. Your volunteer number is shared with coordinators and the volunteer WhatsApp group.
  • Faculty accounts, created by the Bridges team, can browse and open contacts like members, but aren’t listed themselves.
  • Service providers that host the site, handle Google sign-in, send email and process payments handle data for us under contract. We never sell or rent data, and we don’t share it with advertisers.

6. How long we keep it

  • Certificates are deleted 15 days after approval, or 30 days after rejection.
  • Your listing stays while your account is active.
  • When you delete your account, your listing disappears at once and your data is erased within 30 days, except records the law requires us to keep, such as contribution receipts.

7. Your rights

  • Access: download a copy of your data from My profile.
  • Correction: change your details at any time with Edit profile.
  • Erasure: delete your account from My profile.
  • Nomination: name someone to exercise these rights for you if you can’t.
  • Grievances: write to our grievance officer (below).

8. Age

You must be 18 or older to sign up, or have verifiable consent from a parent or guardian.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit, access is limited to the people who need it, and volunteers only ever see redacted files.

10. Grievance officer

[Name], directorybridges@gmail.com. We acknowledge complaints within 24 hours and aim to resolve them within 15 days. If you’re not satisfied, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

11. Changes

We’ll post updates here, and tell you by email or on the site before any significant change takes effect.
