Community guidelines

Everyone here is giving their time for free. These guidelines keep it worth giving.

Last updated 15 November 2026

When you reach out

  • Be specific. Say who you are, what you’re working on and what you need. “Can you look at my portfolio?” gets fewer replies than a clear question.
  • Respect people’s time. Send one message and follow up once. If someone says no or doesn’t reply, let it go.
  • Keep it about design education and careers. No sales, promotions or recruiting for unrelated work.

On your listing

  • Be honest about where you studied and what you do.
  • Only tick what you’re actually happy to help with, and update it when that changes.

Always

  • Don’t share anyone’s contact details or messages outside the platform without their permission.
  • No harassment, discrimination or hate, in any form.

Reporting

Write to directorybridges@gmail.com. We review every report within 3 days, and the person isn’t told who reported them.

What happens

Depending on what happened: a warning, a lower contact limit, or removal from the directory.
