About

A few steps ahead, one message away

Bridges.directory is a free, verified directory of students and alumni from India’s design schools. It exists so that anyone preparing for an entrance, building a portfolio or choosing a specialisation can ask someone who has already done it.

Who’s listed

Current students and alumni of all 19 NIFT campuses, the NIDs, and the IITs that teach design. Everyone is verified before they appear, so the person you write to really did study where they say.

How verification works

  • Students get a one-time code at their institute email. Anyone without one can upload a bonafide certificate instead.
  • Alumni get a one-time code at their alumni email, where their institute gives one. Anyone without one can upload a degree certificate or alumni ID, or enter a referral code from a verified alum of the same institute.
  • Certificates are checked by two student volunteers. They see only your name, institute, branch and year; everything else is hidden, and the file is deleted 15 days after approval, or 30 days after rejection.

How contact works

Signed-in members can open a limited number of contacts. The limit, and when it resets, is shown in your account. The limit keeps inboxes manageable for the people who help, so please make each message count.

Who runs it

A small team of design students, with current students volunteering to review certificates. Running costs are about ₹10,000 a year, covered by contributions from members. There are no ads, and we never sell data.

Get in touch

Write to directorybridges@gmail.com. To report a listing or a safety concern, write to directorybridges@gmail.com.
