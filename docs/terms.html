Terms of use

The rules for using Bridges.directory. Creating an account means you agree to them and to the Privacy policy.

Last updated 15 November 2026

1. Who can join

  • Current students and alumni of the institutes listed on the site. Faculty accounts are created by the Bridges team on request.
  • You must be 18 or older, or have consent from a parent or guardian.
  • One account per person, and everything in your listing must be true.

2. Verification and referrals

We may refuse or remove any listing we can’t verify. Refer only people you know studied at your institute. Misusing referrals can lead to both accounts being removed.

3. Using contact details

Use them only to ask for help, advice or opportunities related to design education and careers. Don’t:

  • send bulk or copy-pasted messages, or add people to mailing lists;
  • copy, scrape, sell or publish anyone’s details;
  • promote products, services or unrelated schemes;
  • harass anyone, or keep writing after they’ve said no.

The contact limit is part of these terms. Don’t try to get around it.

4. Volunteers

Keep everything you see while reviewing confidential. Decide honestly, and don’t download, copy or share certificates.

5. Your listing

You’re responsible for what you add, and you allow us to show it to signed-in members. You can edit or delete it at any time.

6. Contributions

Contributions are voluntary and pay for running costs; they don’t buy anything. They’re refundable only for duplicate or mistaken payments reported within 7 days. They’re not tax-deductible unless we say so in writing.

7. Suspension

We may limit or suspend accounts that break these terms or the Community guidelines, and we’ll tell you why.

8. No guarantees

Bridges.directory connects people. We don’t guarantee that anyone will reply, and advice from members is their own. Nothing here promises admission, a job or an internship.

9. Liability

As far as the law allows, we aren’t liable for indirect losses, and our total liability to you is limited to what you contributed in the previous 12 months.

10. Law

These terms are governed by the laws of India, and the courts at [city, to be confirmed] have jurisdiction.

11. Changes and contact

We’ll post updates here and tell you about significant ones. Questions: directorybridges@gmail.com.
