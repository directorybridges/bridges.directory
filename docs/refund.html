Refund and cancellation policy

Contributions are voluntary and one-time. Here is what that means if something goes wrong.

Last updated 15 November 2026

What a contribution is

A contribution is a voluntary, one-time payment towards the running costs of Bridges.directory. It doesn’t buy a product, a service or a subscription, so there is nothing to cancel and nothing is charged again.

When we refund

Contributions are refundable only for duplicate or mistaken payments reported within 7 days. Write to directorybridges@gmail.com with the date, the amount and the payment reference.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method. Your bank or card provider may take a few working days to show it.

Tax

Contributions are not tax-deductible unless we say so in writing.
