What we store

Cookies and similar browser storage are small pieces of data kept on your device. Here’s everything Bridges.directory stores:

NameWhyKept for
bridges-sessionEssentialKeeps you logged in.Until you log out
Google sign-inEssentialSet by Google while you sign in, so it can confirm your account. Covered by Google’s own privacy policy.Set by Google
bridges-themeEssentialRemembers light, dark or system appearance.Until you change it
bridges-consentEssentialRemembers your cookie choice, so we don’t ask again.12 months
AnalyticsOptionalAnonymous page counts. No cross-site tracking, and IP addresses aren’t stored.Only if you allow it

Your choice

Essential storage is needed for the site to work, so it’s always on. Analytics stay off until you allow them. Change your mind any time with Cookie settings at the bottom of every page, or clear this site’s data in your browser.
